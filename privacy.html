Kadoki privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026

This policy covers the Kadoki Android app (app.kadoki), made by RP&S. Questions or requests: n.cirit.n@gmail.com.

On this phone

Your box designs, daily boxes, drafts and the gifts you send and receive (with their photos, sounds and videos) are stored on this phone. Without an account nothing of yours leaves it.

With an account

You sign in with Google. We keep your name, photo and email address from Google to run your account. Your friends see your name and photo. After you sign in we ask for your birthday once: Kadoki is for people 13 and older, and some daily surprises are only for adults. Your birthday is kept with your account, private to you, and never shown to friends. Gifts you send are stored online, with their media, so your friends can receive them; media is compressed on your phone first. We also keep a notification token for each phone you use, so we can tell you when a gift arrives or is opened.

Daily quotas, ads and Kadoki Plus

A free account can send a few gifts a day and gets box spins and design rights; our server counts them with your account and starts over at your midnight. If you choose to watch an ad for more, the ad comes from Google AdMob. Where the law asks for it, you choose first whether ads may be personalized, and you can change that later (Profile and settings → Plus, ads and today's quota → Ad privacy choices). AdMob may use your phone's advertising ID and IP address to show ads and count them, and with your consent to personalize them. We give AdMob only your account's random id and the reward you picked, so our server can grant it; we don't get information about you from AdMob. Kadoki Plus is bought through Google Play: Google takes the payment and we never see your card. Our server checks each purchase with Google Play and keeps whether your Plus is active and when it renews or ends.

Anonymous gifts

When you send a gift anonymously, the receiver never sees who sent it. Our server still knows, so it can tell you when the gift is opened and so blocks and reports work.

Links and videos

When you add a link, our server fetches its title and picture once, when the gift is sent, so the receiver's phone doesn't contact the site until they open the link. YouTube videos play in YouTube's own player, which connects to YouTube when you press play.

Blocking and reporting

You can remove or block a friend at any time. Blocked people can't send you gifts or invites, and they aren't told. When you report a gift, it disappears from your shelf and we keep a copy, with its media, so we can review it. Reporting an anonymous gift can block its sender without telling you who they are.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account removes your profile, birthday, friends, blocks, notification tokens, quotas and every gift you sent or received online, with their media. Reports you made are kept for review without your name. Gifts already downloaded to someone else's phone stay on that phone until they remove them. Your designs, drafts and daily boxes on this phone stay unless you uninstall the app. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Kadoki Plus subscription; cancel it in Google Play. Without the app, you can ask us to delete your account here.

What we don't do

No tracking or analytics, and we never sell your data. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one, and never for Kadoki Plus. We use Google Firebase to store and deliver gifts, Google AdMob for the ads you choose to watch and Google Play for Kadoki Plus.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date, and tell you in the app for important changes.

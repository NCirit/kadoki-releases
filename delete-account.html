Delete your Kadoki account

In the app

Open Kadoki, go to Settings → Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email n.cirit.n@gmail.com from the Google account you sign in to Kadoki with, and ask us to delete your account. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

Your profile (name, photo, email), birthday, friends, blocks, notification tokens, quotas, your Kadoki Plus status and every gift you sent or received online, with their photos, sounds and videos.

What is kept

Reports you made are kept for review without your name. Gifts already downloaded to someone else's phone stay on that phone until they remove them. Your designs, drafts and daily boxes on your own phone stay until you uninstall the app. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Kadoki Plus subscription; cancel it in Google Play.
